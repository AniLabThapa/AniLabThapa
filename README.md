## 🏛️ About Me

I am **Anil Thapa**, currently pursuing a Master's degree in **Artificial Intelligence at Gachon University, South Korea**, where I work as a **Research Assistant in the Financial Data Science Laboratory**.

My research focuses on the intersection of **financial econometrics, financial time-series analysis, multifractal methods, statistical learning, and data-driven decision-making**.

My current work explores:

- **Financial time-series analysis** and quantitative financial modeling
- **Long-memory, nonlinear, and multiscale dynamics** in financial data
- **Multifractal and cross-correlation methods** for studying financial dependence
- **Machine learning and structure-aware learning** for financial data
- **Data-driven approaches to forecasting, risk analysis, and financial decision-making**

I am particularly interested in understanding how statistical and mathematical structure in financial time series can be incorporated into modern learning methods rather than treating prediction and decision-making as purely black-box problems.

## 🔬 Current Research

### Differentiable Multifractal-DFA: A Structure-Preserving Regularizer for Decision-Focused Portfolio Learning

My current master's research investigates how **multifractal structure in financial time series can be incorporated into machine-learning objectives**.

The project combines:

- Multifractal-DFA
- Differentiable regularization
- Decision-Focused Learning
- Mean–Variance Optimization
- Neural networks
- Automatic differentiation

The goal is to investigate whether preserving multiscale fluctuation characteristics during learning can provide useful information for **financial return/risk prediction and downstream portfolio decisions**.
